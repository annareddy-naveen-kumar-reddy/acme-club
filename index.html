WHO WE ARE

About ACME Club

[Add ACME Club introduction and background here]

ACME (Association of Communication Majors and Enthusiasts) is the flagship professional and technical student organization under the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), School of Engineering, Mohan Babu University.

We cultivate a vibrant ecosystem of student innovators, researchers, and engineers exploring communication systems, signal processing, VLSI, IoT, embedded systems, and next-gen AI technologies.

Our Core Vision

"To inspire, mentor, and empower future engineering leaders through collaborative projects, technical workshops, industry exposure, and groundbreaking research."
